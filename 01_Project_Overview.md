# Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

The Banking Analytics Project is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing banking operations and customer behavior.

The project simulates a real-world analytical environment in which raw operational data must be transformed into reliable information for business decision-making.

The project covers the complete workflow from database preparation and data validation to business analysis and business intelligence reporting.

---

## 2. Business Objective

The primary objective is to understand the bank's customers, financial activity, operational performance, loan portfolio, card activity, fraud exposure, and customer support performance.

The analysis aims to answer business questions and identify actionable opportunities rather than simply producing visual reports.

---

## 3. Analytical Areas

The project focuses on six main analytical areas:

### Customer Analytics

- Customer segmentation
- High-value customers
- Customer activity
- Customer retention indicators

### Transaction Analytics

- Transaction volume
- Deposit and withdrawal behavior
- Transaction channels
- Transaction trends

### Branch Analytics

- Branch activity
- Transaction performance
- Customer and account distribution

### Loan Analytics

- Loan portfolio
- Loan status
- Repayment activity
- Interest analysis
- Default risk indicators

### Card & Fraud Analytics

- Card distribution
- Card activity
- Credit exposure
- Fraud detection

### Customer Support Analytics

- Ticket volume
- Resolution time
- SLA performance
- Customer satisfaction

---

## 4. Analytical Approach

The project follows a structured approach:

1. Understand the business requirements.
2. Inspect the available data.
3. Build the relational database.
4. Validate data quality.
5. Perform exploratory SQL analysis.
6. Build reusable business logic.
7. Prepare data for reporting.
8. Design the semantic model.
9. Develop analytical metrics.
10. Communicate insights and recommendations.

---

## 5. Expected Outcome

The final solution will provide a centralized analytical view of banking operations and help stakeholders understand:

- Who the most valuable customers are.
- How customers use banking products.
- How transaction behavior changes over time.
- Which branches perform best.
- How healthy the loan portfolio is.
- Where fraud risk is concentrated.
- How effectively customer issues are resolved.
